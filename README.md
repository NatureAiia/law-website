## About

The **School of Law website** is a dedicated online platform showcasing the School, its programmes, activities, achievements, and impact. It highlights important practical initiatives such as the **Law Clinic** and its community outreach programmes, showing how students and lecturers use legal knowledge to serve and support communities.

Visitors can:

- Explore the different law programmes
- Meet faculty members
- Access news and upcoming events
- Learn about opportunities available within the School
- Browse the Law Clinic section — outreach activities, legal awareness programmes, community engagement, and impact — along with information for people who may need legal assistance

Overall, the website creates a professional and accessible digital identity for the School of Law, making it easier for **prospective students, current students, alumni, legal professionals, partners, and members of the community** to connect with the School and stay informed about its work.

> It's about showcasing the Law Clinic and its programmes to the world.
